## The τ−β non-vacuity statement (the part the paper under-specifies)

Prop 1 (bouncer.tex:362-388) guarantees the victim floor `r^π0 + τ − β` only while
`τ − β > 0`. The paper says β>0 from hot/cold/conflict skew and that reseeding
"shrinks it toward the i.i.d. value" (bouncer.tex:394-397) but never gives β(σ_het).
I derived and measured it.

**Per-epoch bias injected into Δ̂_R by N(0,σ_het) per-set offsets has two parts:**

1. **Systematic (mean) bias → killed by reseeding.** With `reseed_period=1`
   (set_dueling.py:38,68-69) the L/F partition is redrawn every epoch from the
   secret hw RNG, so the offsets favoring Leader-L this epoch favor Leader-F next.
   Measured mean over 5000 epochs: −0.00065 at σ=0.10, −0.00196 at σ=0.30 —
   i.e. ≈0. **This is exactly the "averaging down" the paper claims, and it is real
   for the time-averaged CUSUM drift.** A *frozen* (non-reseeded) single assignment
   instead locks in a static bias of −0.0139 (σ=0.10) … −0.0416 (σ=0.30): reseeding
   buys ~20× on the systematic term.

2. **Per-epoch fluctuation (RMS) → NOT killed by reseeding.** Each epoch's bias is a
   fresh i.i.d. draw with
   **β_rms(σ_het) = σ_het·√(1/n_L + 1/n_F) = 0.25·σ_het** (n_L=n_F=32, coef=0.25 exact).
   Measured RMS matches the closed form to 3 dp:

   | σ_het | β_rms measured | β_rms closed-form (0.25·σ) | τ−β_rms (τ=0.05) |
   |-------|----------------|----------------------------|------------------|
   | 0.05  | 0.0126 | 0.0125 | +0.0374 |
   | 0.10  | 0.0251 | 0.0250 | +0.0249 |
   | 0.15  | 0.0377 | 0.0375 | +0.0123 |
   | 0.20  | 0.0502 | 0.0500 | **−0.0002** |
   | 0.30  | 0.0754 | 0.0750 | −0.0254 |

**Non-vacuity threshold:** `τ − β_rms > 0  ⟺  σ_het < τ/0.25 = 4τ = 0.20`.
At the paper's τ=0.05 and n_L=n_F=32, **Prop 1's margin is non-vacuous for
σ_het < 0.20, and goes negative at the very top of the swept range (σ_het=0.20–0.30).**

## Why does end-to-end TPR stay 1.0 even where τ−β_rms ≤ 0?

Because reseeding moves the binding constraint from the *instantaneous* β_rms to the
*CUSUM-averaged* β. CUSUM integrates over a window of K epochs (gate_fsm); the
fluctuation term averages as β_rms/√K while the systematic term is already ~0. So the
**effective β on the detector statistic is β_rms/√K, not β_rms.** The mimicry/broad
attacks in the sweep push a large persistent (1−f)·g signal that dwarfs the residual,
so TPR=1.0 survives — but the *latency* cost (1.0→1.7 windows) is precisely the price
of needing more epochs to average the σ=0.30 fluctuation down below threshold. The
latency drift is the honest fingerprint of β biting; TPR is saturated and hides it.

## Honest assessment / recommended claim adjustment

- **Defensible (defended=2):** "Under set heterogeneity up to σ_het=0.30, end-to-end
  detection (TPR 1.0), clean FPR (0), and the steady floor (0.60–0.64%) are preserved;
  only latency drifts 1.0→1.7 windows." This reproduces and the mechanism (reseeding
  zeroes the *systematic* bias; CUSUM averages the residual *fluctuation*) is verified.

- **Overclaim to AVOID:** stating or implying reseeding makes β vanish / drives the
  Prop-1 margin non-vacuous for all σ_het. It does NOT. Reseeding zeroes only the
  *mean* bias; the per-epoch fluctuation β_rms=0.25·σ_het is untouched and crosses
  τ=0.05 at σ_het=0.20. The *instantaneous* per-epoch Prop-1 guarantee is vacuous for
  σ_het ≥ 0.20.

- **Recommended paper edit:** add the closed form β_rms = σ_het√(1/n_L+1/n_F) and state
  the non-vacuity condition explicitly: σ_het < τ·√(n_L n_F/(n_L+n_F)) = 4τ = 0.20 at
  the standard config. Note that end-to-end survival past this point is purchased by
  CUSUM averaging (effective β ≈ β_rms/√K), at a latency cost — which is exactly what
  Fig. robust_env shows. This converts the hand-wave at bouncer.tex:394-397 into a
  quantitative, falsifiable bound and frames the latency drift as the predicted cost.

## Re-verification (this hardening pass)
Independent re-run of `exp_robust_env.py` reproduces the table above to the dp shown.
Closed-form β_rms re-derived at the **actual** harness config `n_sets=2048, n_L=n_F=32`
(experiments/common.py:41 STD), where the finite-population correction √((N−n)/(N−1))=0.992
≈1, so β_rms=0.25·σ_het is exact to 3 dp (measured 0.0125/0.0250/0.0375/0.0500/0.0750 at
σ=0.05/0.10/0.15/0.20/0.30 over 2×10⁵ epochs). Systematic mean E[b_R]=+7e-5 (σ=0.10) …
+2e-4 (σ=0.30) — i.e. reseeding zeroes it to within Monte-Carlo error. Crossover τ−β_rms=0
lands at σ_het*=4τ=0.2008 (measured), confirming non-vacuity for σ_het<0.20.
**Reseeding is load-bearing, quantified:** with a *frozen* partition (no reseed), the
single per-episode draw |b_R| exceeds τ=0.05 in **32.7%** of episodes at σ=0.20 (persistent
DC the CUSUM cannot average away); reseeding converts this into zero-mean per-epoch noise.

## Connection to N2
N2's binding caveat #2 stated β is "ADDITIVE on σ_R and does NOT shrink with |R|;
true δ_R ≈ k·σ_R + β." N3 quantifies that β: per-epoch β_rms = σ_het·√(1/n_L+1/n_F).
Note it shrinks with the *leader count* (1/√n_L), not with |R| — consistent with N2's
FEAS result that leader count, not region size, sets resolution. β_rms is the floor on
δ_R that no amount of |R| choice removes; only spending more leaders (n_L↑) or more
epochs (CUSUM K↑) reduces it.
