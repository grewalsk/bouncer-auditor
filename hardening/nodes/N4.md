## Result (results/coverage_hole.json, figures/coverage_hole.pdf)

**Money trace (the primary deliverable).** A slow-bleed adversary applies a constant per-window
competence harm `h = 0.06` to a victim region `R` (256 sets), applied to ALL region sets (the
secret is intact, so it cannot spare the in-region leaders). Over a 360-window horizon:
- the gate **never fires** (`first_gated after onset = None`),
- `Δ̂_R` stays at mean **0.205 ≥ τ = 0.05** (and above the CUSUM reference `K = 0.10`), so the
  one-sided CUSUM never accumulates,
- yet **cumulative victim harm grows monotonically to 19.2** — unbounded in the horizon.

**Evade-forever.** Sweeping the horizon `T ∈ {120,…,600}`, the attack evades at every length
and total harm scales linearly (`harm ∝ T`): the per-window harm sits permanently below the
detectable floor, so longer deployment = strictly more undetected damage.

**The resolution floor scales with budget (closed form).** `δ_R^min(B) = k·σ_R`,
`σ_R = r_max√((1/4m)(2/B))` with `k = 3`: `B=4 → 0.188`, `B=8 → 0.133`, `B=16 → 0.094`,
`B=32 → 0.066` — i.e. `δ_R^min ∝ 1/√B`. A larger per-region leader budget shrinks the hole but
never closes it; any finite `B` leaves a band `h < δ_R^min(B)` the adversary can bleed in
forever. (We report this as the *closed-form* prediction from N2; we do not over-rely on the
finite-horizon empirical cliff, which at fixed `(K,H)` is confounded by per-budget false-alarm
calibration.)

**Verdict.** Confirming evidence for the scoped claim and the named open vulnerability. The
manuscript now carries this figure alongside the closed-form coverage-hole paragraph in
the mimicry-resistance proposition.
