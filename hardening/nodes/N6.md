## Bouncer contribution under test (paper/bouncer.tex L118-142)
Three load-bearing pillars:
1. **Secret set-dueling substrate** — repurpose DIP/DRRIP dedicated-sets+counter from policy *selection* to policy *trust*; assignment kept **secret**.
2. **Realized-competence audit** — Δ̂ = r̄_L − r̄_F (counterfactual-free reward deficit), explicitly *not* input-side OOD/drift.
3. **Bounded-regret floor + mimicry-resistance** — Lemma (CUSUM ARL-tied regret floor) + Proposition (mimicry resistance = secrecy of sampler), applied to a *learned microarchitectural controller*.

The conjunction "secret reward-dueling competence auditor with a regret floor for a learned microarch controller" is what must be unique.

## Collision analysis on the unique conjunction
- **Secret/randomized auditing of a learned controller**: closest analog found is LLM-inference verification via randomized sampling (arXiv 2511.02620) — different domain (weight-exfiltration detection), but it *independently validates the secrecy principle* ("if verified messages are predictable, an adversary selectively encodes into unverified outputs"). This is an argument FOR Bouncer's secrecy proposition, not a prior that scoops it. No microarchitectural or competence-dueling instance exists.
- **Competence (realized-reward) vs input-OOD distinction**: the OOD/drift literature is explicitly input-side; Bouncer's reframing to realized competence is defensible and uncontested.
- No prior unifies (secret set-dueling substrate) × (realized competence) × (regret floor) × (learned microarch controller). The contribution stands.

## Honest caveats / weakening guidance (do NOT overclaim)
- The "first to" should be scoped: claim novelty of the **conjunction**, not novelty of any single ingredient (set-dueling, CUSUM, simplex, safe-RL bootstrap are all prior). The current tex already does this correctly ("repurposes," "borrows the substrate") — keep that framing.
- Add the two 2026/2021 neighbors above so a reviewer cannot say the RTA-of-adapting-controllers and black-box-Simplex literature was missed. This is a citation hardening, not a contribution change.
- Mimicry-resistance proposition's strength is conditional on assignment secrecy surviving side channels — out of scope for N6, flagged for the threat-model node.
